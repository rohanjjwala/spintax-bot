# Humanize: clean up AI-sounding copy

Rule-based rewrite that removes the patterns that make cold email read as machine-written: stock openers, dashes, filler, inflated vocabulary and pushy sign-offs. Merge tags and the signature are protected. See `skills/cold-email-humanizer/` for the full rule set.

Generated by `scripts/generate-examples.js` from `examples/inputs/`. Every example below was checked: 1,000 random renders with 0 grammar-lint hits, and the spintax parses back cleanly for its platform.

## Example 1: saas ai draft

**Before**

```text
Hi {{first_name}},

I hope this email finds you well! I wanted to reach out because I came across {{company_name}} and was truly impressed by your innovative approach to leveraging cutting-edge technology.

Our robust platform seamlessly streamlines your sales workflow — it's a true game-changer for teams like yours.

Please don't hesitate to reach out if you'd like to delve deeper into this.

Best,
%signature%
```

**After**

```text
Hi {{first_name}},

I'm writing because I came across {{company_name}} and was impressed by your new approach to using modern technology.

Our solid platform smoothly simplifies your sales workflow. It's a big change for teams like yours.

Reply anytime.

Best,
%signature%
```

**What changed:** Removed the stock opener · Shortened "wanted to reach out" · Replaced "don't hesitate" · Cut "truly" · AI word: leverage · AI word: streamline · AI word: seamlessly · AI word: robust · AI word: cutting-edge · AI word: innovative · AI word: game-changer · Split a dash into two sentences
