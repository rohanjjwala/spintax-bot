# Mixed spintax at 50%

**Mode:** Word swaps inside the body, plus whole-sentence blocks for the lines everyone writes: the CTA, the opt-out, the follow-up bump and the sign-off.

**Intensity:** About half of the phrases the knowledge base knows get a block.

Generated by `scripts/generate-examples.js` from `examples/inputs/`. Every example below was checked: 1,000 random renders with 0 grammar-lint hits, and the spintax parses back cleanly for its platform.
