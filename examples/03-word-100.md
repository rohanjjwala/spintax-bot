# Word-level spintax at 100%

**Mode:** Swaps words and short phrases inside each sentence, like `I {noticed|saw}`. Meaning and length stay the same. Merge tags, the signature, links and numbers are never spun.

**Intensity:** Every phrase the knowledge base knows gets a block, with up to four options each.

Generated by `scripts/generate-examples.js` from `examples/inputs/`. Every example below was checked: 1,000 random renders with 0 grammar-lint hits, and the spintax parses back cleanly for its platform.
