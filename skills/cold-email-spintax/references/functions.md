# The 12 functions

One section per function. Each has the exact definition, what counts as an eligible phrase, how many options per block, what not to vary, and two input and output examples copied verbatim from `examples/examples.json` (the same data as the files in `examples/`). Every example was generated by the rule engine and checked with 1,000 random renders and zero lint hits.

Intensity sets two things at once: how many of the eligible phrases get a block, and how many options each block gets.

| Intensity | Share of eligible phrases spun | Options per block |
|---|---|---|
| 50 | about half, spread evenly through the email | 2 |
| 80 | most | 3 (2 where the group only has 2) |
| 100 | all | up to 4 (never more than the group has) |

"Eligible phrase" always means: a word or short phrase from `phrase-bank.md` (or one that passes the same tests) that is not protected, not a number, not a proper noun, and passes the context rules for that phrase.

## 1. word-50: Word-level spintax at 50 percent

Definition: swap individual words and short phrases inside sentences. Sentence structure never changes. About half of the eligible phrases get a block, chosen so the blocks are spread through the email rather than bunched in one paragraph. Two options per block, the original first.

Eligible: any phrase-bank entry that passes its context rule. The greeting word ("Hi") counts as an eligible phrase but at 50 percent it is often left alone. "Thanks," as a sign-off is spun as a word ("{Thanks|Thank you},") rather than as a sign-off line.

Options per block: 2.

Do not vary: merge tags, signature, links, numbers, proper nouns, the subject line's variables, the call-to-action sentence as a whole (word swaps inside it are fine), anything on the never-swap list.

Example A (Smartlead):

Input:

<!-- verified: word-50 item 6 input -->
```text
Hi {{first_name}},

Just following up on my last note in case it got buried.

I still have the list of 25 accounts ready for {{company_name}}.

Want me to send it over?

Thanks,
%signature%
```

Output:

<!-- verified: word-50 item 6 output -->
```text
Hi {{first_name}},

Just {following up|checking back} on my last note in case it got buried.

I still have the list of 25 accounts ready for {{company_name}}.

Want me to send it over?

{Thanks|Thank you},
%signature%
```

Example B (EmailBison):

Input:

<!-- verified: word-50 item 2 input -->
```text
Subject: {FIRST_NAME}, after-hours calls

Hey {FIRST_NAME},

Saw {COMPANY_NAME} is hiring a dispatcher in {CITY}. Usually that means the phones are full and some calls go to voicemail after 5pm.

We help HVAC shops answer every call and book the job, even at night.

Worth a quick chat this week?

Thanks,
{SENDER_EMAIL_SIGNATURE}
```

Output:

<!-- verified: word-50 item 2 output -->
```text
Subject: {FIRST_NAME}, after-hours calls

{Saw|Noticed} {COMPANY_NAME} is hiring a dispatcher in {CITY}. Usually that means the phones are full and {some|a few} calls go to voicemail after 5pm.

We help HVAC shops answer every call and book the job, even at night.

Worth {a quick|a brief} chat this week?

{Thanks|Thank you},
{SENDER_EMAIL_SIGNATURE}
```

## 2. word-80: Word-level spintax at 80 percent

Definition: same as word-50, but most eligible phrases get a block and each block carries three options where the group has three. The greeting word almost always gets a block at this level: `{Hi|Hello|Hey} {{first_name}},`.

Eligible: as word-50.

Options per block: 3 (2 where the group has only 2, for example contractions, "because | since", "book | schedule").

Do not vary: as word-50.

Example A (Smartlead):

Input:

```text
Just following up on my last note in case it got buried.

I still have the list of 25 accounts ready for {{company_name}}.

Thanks,
%signature%
```

Output:

<!-- verified: word-80 item 6 output -->

```text
Just {following up|checking back} on my last {note|message|email} in case it got buried.

I still have the list of 25 accounts ready for {{company_name}}.

Want me to send it over?

{Thanks|Thank you},
%signature%
```

## 3. word-100: Word-level spintax at 100 percent

Definition: every eligible phrase gets a block. Up to four options per block, which in practice means three for most groups and four only where the bank has four. Sentence structure still never changes.

Eligible: as word-50. At 100 percent the contraction pairs ("I am | I'm", "we will | we'll") are all in play, so check the register: a casual email with every contraction expanded reads stiff. Pick the direction that matches the sender.

Options per block: up to 4.

Do not vary: as word-50. At this level the temptation is to spin "quick fix", "cold email" and "growing rapidly". Do not. See the never-swap list.

Example A (Smartlead):

```text
%signature%
```

## 4. sentence-50: Sentence-level spintax at 50 percent

Definition: wrap whole sentences in a single block that holds complete rewrites of the sentence. About half of the eligible sentences get a block. Two versions per block, the original first.

Eligible sentence: a sentence that contains no merge tag, link, signature tag or existing block, and either (a) is a known intent (greeting word, call to action, send offer, interest check, opt-out, follow-up bump, show-how-it-works, sign-off) or (b) contains at least one eligible phrase, so a rewrite can be built from word swaps.

How a rewrite is built: for intent sentences use the curated alternatives in `phrase-bank.md`. For any other sentence, change two or three eligible phrases at once and keep everything else, so the rewrite is the same sentence with the same facts.

Sentences that contain a merge tag, link or signature: cannot be wrapped (no platform parses a tag inside a block). Give them word-level swaps instead, as in the first sentence of Example B below.

Options per block: 2.

Do not vary: the facts, numbers and names inside the sentence; sentence order; paragraph breaks.

Example A (Smartlead):

Input:

<!-- verified: sentence-50 item 6 input -->
```text
Hi {{first_name}},

Just following up on my last note in case it got buried.

I still have the list of 25 accounts ready for {{company_name}}.

Want me to send it over?

Thanks,
%signature%
```

Output:

<!-- verified: sentence-50 item 6 output -->
```text
Hi {{first_name}},

{Just following up on my last note in case it got buried.|Following up on my last note.}

I still have the list of 25 accounts ready for {{company_name}}.

Want me to send it over?

{Thanks,|Best,}
%signature%
```

## 5. sentence-80: Sentence-level spintax at 80 percent

Definition: as sentence-50, but most eligible sentences get a block and each block holds three versions. The greeting word gets a word block (`{Hi|Hello|Hey}`) because the variable next to it cannot go in a sentence block.

Eligible: as sentence-50.

Options per block: 3.

Do not vary: as sentence-50. Watch the rewrites: each one must still read as a sentence a person would send. Reject any rewrite that swaps a word into the wrong meaning (see the engine weaknesses list in `phrase-bank.md`).

## 10. humanize: Clean up AI-sounding copy

Definition: rewrite copy that reads as machine-written so it reads as a person wrote it, without changing what it says. No spintax is added. The full pattern catalogue is in the sibling skill `cold-email-humanizer`.

What gets changed: stock openers ("I hope this email finds you well"), scene-setting ("In today's fast-paced landscape"), filler ("just wanted to", "in order to", "truly", "actually"), inflated vocabulary (leverage, seamless, robust, cutting-edge, game-changer, unlock, elevate, synergy, delve, empower, streamline), "not just X, it's Y" lines, lists of three abstractions, "ensuring ..." tails, gushing praise, pushy closers ("don't miss out", "reserve your spot today"), em and en dashes, exclamation marks, curly quotes. A line that becomes empty after a removal is dropped, not left as a blank.

What never changes: merge tags, signature tags, links, every fact, number and name, the ask (the call to action keeps its intent even when its wording changes), line and paragraph structure.

Output: the rewritten email only, then a one-line list of what changed.

A rule engine does this with find-and-replace, which is why its output below is clean but flat. A model should do better: see the gold rewrites in `cold-email-humanizer/references/examples.md`.

Example A:

Input:

<!-- verified: humanize item 6 input -->
```text
Hi {{first_name}},

I just wanted to circle back on my previous email. I understand you're incredibly busy, so I'll keep this brief.

Simply put, we can help {{company_name}} supercharge its pipeline — it's that simple.

Let me know your thoughts!

%signature%
```

Output:

<!-- verified: humanize item 6 output -->
```text
Hi {{first_name}},

I wanted to follow up on my previous email. I'll keep this short.

We can help {{company_name}} grow its pipeline.

Let me know your thoughts.

%signature%
```

Example B:

Input:

<!-- verified: humanize item 3 input -->
```text
Hi {{first_name}},

I hope this message finds you well. I noticed that {{company_name}} is growing rapidly, which is truly exciting!

As a testament to our commitment to excellence, we have helped numerous organizations navigate the complex landscape of hiring.

Feel free to reach out at your earliest convenience.

Best regards,
%signature%
```

Output:

<!-- verified: humanize item 3 output -->
```text

I noticed that {{company_name}} is growing rapidly, which is exciting.

We have helped many organizations with hiring.

Reply anytime.

Best regards,
%signature%
```

## 11. humanize-then-spin: Humanize, then word-level spintax at 80 percent

Definition: the web app's default. Run the humanize pass first, then spin the humanized text word-level at 80 percent (three options per block, most eligible phrases). The order matters: spinning first and humanizing second produces options the humanizer cannot see.

Eligible: as word-80, applied to the humanized text.

Options per block: 3.

Do not vary: as word-80. Also keep the humanizer's replacements stable: if "leverage" became "use", do not then spin "use" into something that reintroduces the problem.

Output: the spun email, optionally one render, then a summary line that lists the humanize changes and the spin stats.

Example A (Smartlead):

Input:


## 12. convert: Convert spintax between platforms

Definition: rewrite existing spin blocks, merge tags and the signature tag into the target platform's syntax. Nothing else changes: no block added, removed, reordered or reworded; no text edited.

Rules:
- Blocks: `{a|b}` to `{{RANDOM | a | b}}` for Instantly, the reverse for EmailBison and Smartlead.
- Variables: map the name across cases (`first_name`, `FIRST_NAME`, `firstName`) and brace styles. Custom variables map the same way (`{CITY}` to `{{city}}`).
- Fallbacks: keep for Instantly, drop for EmailBison and Smartlead and say so.
- Signature: swap the tag, keep the line.
- Links, email addresses, numbers and text: untouched.

Output: the converted email, then one line listing the token changes.

Example A (Smartlead to EmailBison):

Input:

<!-- verified: convert item 6 input -->
```text
{Hi|Hey|Hello} {{first_name}},

{Just following up on my last note in case it got buried.|Floating this back to the top of your inbox.|Following up on my last note.}

I still have the list of 25 accounts ready for {{company_name}}.

{Want me to send it over?|Okay if I pass it along?|Should I send it your way?}

{Thanks,|Best,|Talk soon,}
%signature%
```

Output:

<!-- verified: convert item 6 output -->
```text
{Hi|Hey|Hello} {FIRST_NAME},

{Just following up on my last note in case it got buried.|Floating this back to the top of your inbox.|Following up on my last note.}

I still have the list of 25 accounts ready for {COMPANY_NAME}.

{Want me to send it over?|Okay if I pass it along?|Should I send it your way?}

{Thanks,|Best,|Talk soon,}
```
