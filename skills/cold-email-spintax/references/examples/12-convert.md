# Convert spintax between platforms

Rewrites existing spin blocks, merge tags and the signature tag into the target platform's syntax without adding or removing any variation. Inline fallbacks such as `{{first_name|there}}` are kept for Instantly and dropped (with a note) for EmailBison and Smartlead, which have no inline fallback.

Generated by `scripts/generate-examples.js` from `examples/inputs/`. Every example below was checked: 1,000 random renders with 0 grammar-lint hits, and the spintax parses back cleanly for its platform.
